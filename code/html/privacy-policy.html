Privacy Policy

How CULTURE / 001 collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

When you browse our store, place an order, or contact us, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, delivery address, and order details. We may also collect basic usage information, such as the pages you visit and the device you use, to keep the site working well.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To respond to questions, requests, and support messages.
  • To improve our products, website, and shopping experience.
  • To send updates or offers, only where you have opted in.

Cookies and local storage

Our site uses browser storage to keep track of the items in your cart between visits. This information stays on your device and is not used to identify you personally. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with trusted partners who help us run the store, such as payment processors and delivery services, and only to the extent needed to complete your order or meet a legal obligation.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at hello@culture001.com.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out through our contact page or email hello@culture001.com.
